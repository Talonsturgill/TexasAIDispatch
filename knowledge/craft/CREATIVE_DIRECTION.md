# Story-led cinematic direction

Effective September 29, 2026; config/creative_production.json owns the date and version.
Earlier editions retain their original contracts. This policy replaces the dimensional opening
and runtime quota. All source, quality, independent review, native output, audio, caption,
budget and shipment requirements remain. It adds no score threshold or review role.

## Choose the story from its tangible stakes

Before narration, selected.visual_stakes records affected_person, physical_subject,
visible_change, consequence, opening_question, answer, asset_fit and source_urls. Name the
specific worker, resident or group and what changes for them. Tie the opening question to the
answer that the available evidence can support. A large announcement without useful pictures
is a weaker candidate than a smaller action with a visible consequence.

Inspect the real site, equipment, people, workflow and records during the existing bounded
visual search. Prefer the most informative authentic image. Apply the no-repeat policy in
DAILY_PRODUCTION.md. Use source-still or source-excerpt candidate rows with asset_url and the
same inspection/rights evidence as source-footage. Diagram rows need a sourced relationship
and disclosure Illustration. These are additional media, not permission to invent a workflow.

## Choose the medium from the evidence

Set cinema.version from the new policy. Keep hero_scene_id, optional hero_passage_end_scene_id,
visible_action, human_consequence and source_limit. dimensional_scene_ids is an exact inventory
of the quality_plan scenes using 3D; an empty list is legitimate. Each scene still needs a
filmable subject, action, consequence and source_basis. Geometry is useful when depth or contact
explains something. Footage is useful when the action is actually visible. A still can establish
a real place; an excerpt can expose the decisive words; a diagram can explain a sourced relation.
Do not force a character into a source image or draw a fake room to meet a quota.

The editorial-v1 route accepts each scene's picture with id, medium, event_id, disclosure and
subject (the inspected subject, checked by the critic). Use camera_strategy sourcePicture and
empty planes for this route's non-dimensional scenes. visual_proof.must_show binds picture.id.
Footage/stills/excerpts require file and sha256 from native_media; footprint, provenance and
rights remain mandatory. Footage also supplies trim_start_s, trim_end_s, creator, source_url,
license_url or its existing editorial_excerpt evidence. It plays at original speed with its
original audio muted. Deliberate environmental audio is separately sourced and mixed.

A crop is static x/y percentages. A focus box uses x/y/width/height percentages of the displayed
frame and reveals on event_id. Use it to reveal a real detail, never imply a machine detected it.
Source-excerpt renders the actual inspected page image, preserving its words. Diagram nodes
carry short label and claim_id; two to four nodes appear on the event clock. The source and
numeral gates cover those printed labels. No long paragraphs or substitute dashboards.
Dimensional scenes omit picture and call the existing demonstrated action route; bespoke
source-backed mechanisms retain their admission and native-hero requirements.

Every current scene receives full-resolution normal/subject-removed sample pairs. The existing
proof key without_stage now removes the principal picture for each medium. It never removes
captions or branding to manufacture visible action. A source still/excerpt may have a justified
intentional_hold within the policy duration after the opening; the phone and final lenses still
judge its purpose. Otherwise the principal picture must visibly develop on its event clock.
The hero remains a finished native passage with sound, even when it uses no 3D.

## Compare two openings once

Prepare opening-a.json and opening-b.json beside storyboard.json. Only the first scene, its
quality_plan row and creative_direction.edits row may differ; keep the same duration, body,
assets, question and source limits. Make genuinely different visual approaches, not different
labels over the same picture. Keep both inside the existing source and medium contracts.
The existing independent code critic reviews both in one reserved assignment and returns
opening-a-critic.json and opening-b-critic.json using the ordinary exact concept/renderer/story
bindings. It can reject both before a render is spent. Do not add another reviewer identity.

Run scripts/opening_compare.py once. It reserves one preflight batch, renders exactly two
quarter-scale full animatics with one browser/bundle, and saves openings/comparison.json.
These are inexpensive opening options with the same body, so the chosen full preview is already
available for continuity review. Identical inputs reuse retained evidence; failed attempts stay
charged. A changed batch requires the existing consolidated repair path, never an automatic reset.

The existing exact-phone critic watches both openings, then the chosen whole sequence. It writes
openings/selection.json with comparison_sha256, selected (a or b), director_identity,
reviewer_identity, reason, rejected_reason and blocking_defects. Reasons describe observed
pixels and the opening question. Copy the chosen frozen board to storyboard.json and its film
to preflight.mp4; retain its matching code critique as storyboard_critic.json, inspect that exact
film with preflight_animatic --inspect-only, then record the ordinary phone verdict on those
bytes. The unused option stays as evidence. Neither option authorizes a narration take until
the chosen cut passes the current phone gate. No third concept or extra panel by default.

## Direct performance, sound and cuts together

creative_direction binds policy_sha256 and records viewer_question, visible_answer,
emotional_turn and medium_choice. Its ordered edits rows contain scene_id, enter_on, leave_on,
cut_after_event_id and next_connection. The actual event must complete before the cut; a prose
promise cannot substitute for its clock. Describe the visible instant that earns each cut and the carried subject
or sourced relationship. The final row states the resolved answer and source limit. After any
structural change, review the whole sequence. Measured narration retimes the event clock; do not
add dead air to preserve an arbitrary grid or accelerate the voice to rescue too many words.

creative_direction.sound records perspective, music_arc, voice_arc and cues. Each cue has id,
event_id, role (environment, contact, sonification or quiet), duration_s, intent and provenance.
Use truthful recorded ambience or an explicitly designed effect. Do not pretend a generated
sound was recorded at the actual site. Avoid a hit at every cut. Include one deliberate background
contrast with a quiet cue and gain from the policy contract. The quiet cue reduces the music and
effects bus on its actual visual event; it never mutes, stretches or splices the narration.

Each non-quiet cue has one sfx_events entry with matching id/event_id, wav, source and gain.
The mixer anchors it to the current board event, trims to its declared duration at original
speed, adds short edge fades and refuses undersized or nonfinite audio. It records every cue,
source WAV hash and the exact sound/timing digest in mix.json. Retime the sfx event file with the
board and remix after a changed clock. Old mix evidence fails the release check.

The existing voice director reads this sound arc and the selected opening. It binds
sound_direction_sha256 to the sound object and gives each line specific intent, relative energy
and an exact spoken emphasis phrase. Contrast curiosity, discovery and the final consequence
without a trailer voice. Numeric pause commands stay out of the TTS prompt. One continuous take
and its existing audible soundcheck remain the default; field completion cannot approve a flat
or synthetic read. The sound lens judges actual delivery, masking, sync and contrast in the MP4.

All three final audiovisual lenses and scoring agents remain. The legacy response field
dimensional_action describes the principal picture in any medium. A judge may reject a weak
image, inert diagram, generic read or unearned cut with timestamped evidence; a medium preference
alone is not a defect. Passing quality moves directly to release and verified shipment.
