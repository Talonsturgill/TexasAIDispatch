## Director

Before coding, choose the visual approach from actual source access. Think through four
lenses in the existing director assignment: the comedian finds an observable mismatch;
the dramatist finds pressure and a changed state; the explainer finds the causal relation;
the enthusiast finds a specific surprising detail. Do not invent a joke, conflict or
outcome. Choose exactly two complete treatments within the current comparison allowance.

Write `art_direction` in each board using `config/art_direction.json`. Explain seven palette
roles, contrasting shapes, the recognizable hero silhouette and its finish, motivated
light, focal hierarchy, action timing, and one signature shot tied to an actual event.
Name the current asset and actual subject ids. Use the source image itself when it is the
hero. Give a dimensional scene a `shots[scene_id]` camera position, target, lens and reason;
an event-bound `to` pose is optional. For the illustrated carton route, use `flat_shots`
with native-coordinate offsets, scale and a reason. Numeric shot choices must be executed
by the routed scene, not left as board prose. A custom renderer must consume the same
profile or explain its retained source lighting and implement its relevant choices.

The focal object stays sharp. Depth blur must arise from actual separation and preserve
contact detail. Never add blur, floating shapes, lens flares, camera kicks or orbiting as
automatic cinematic decoration. Hold the camera while an object performs. Move it to reveal
a relation or follow a finite action. Give a completed result a comprehension hold.

## Scene builder and render owner

Use `prompts/roles/scene-builder.md` in the existing code assignment. Build the finished
hero first, then light and stage it before extending the scene. Recognizable geometry,
edges, seams, hardware, texture and grounded shadows beat a field of placeholder primitives.
Authored 2.5D, useful footage and a readable source image can outperform complicated 3D.
Do not restrict the daily story to the demonstration subject or copy reference b-roll.

The shared `ArtDirectionProvider` carries current choices in both entry points. Three.js
`CinematicStage`/`Studio` execute the light recipe and exposure. `directedShot` executes
authored event-bound cameras. The illustrated carton component executes its flat framing,
finished surfaces, occlusion and contact assemblies. Source footage retains original speed
and practical lighting. Existing boards without this profile retain their original defaults.

Every new event specifies a motion curve within its existing measured window: `travel`
accelerates and brakes; `contact` approaches and settles; `linear` serves constant-speed
movement; `smoothstep` is available for a justified gentle transition. `anticipation` and
`settle` reserve fractions inside that window, never new runtime or voice stretching.
`landing` has a small overshoot and needs a free-prop reason; never use it for a constrained
contact, a foot planted on ground, or a source-implied machine limit. Check music and foley
against the executed contact, not the old curve. No incidental sway substitutes for action.

## Existing independent critic and three scorers

Watch the actual muted sequence first. Name the object, action, changed state and answered
question before reading the profile. Then test whether its choices are visible: compare
silhouette, contact, surface finish, hierarchy, framing, rhythm and hold at phone size and
native crop scale. Inspect the beginning, contact and end of every action. Keep narration
and labels covered on the first pass. A good explanation in the profile cannot clear an
occluded grip, generic slab, unrecognizable subject or absent transformation.

Read `knowledge/craft/cinematic_reference_bank.json`. Its frozen passages teach specific
decisions; their old scores use different rubrics and are not comparable ratings. They are
references, never production assets or current approval. Compare two current complete cuts,
describe a preference with exact times, then record mandatory defects under the existing
criteria. A different palette preference alone does not create another correction. Retain
all original rejections. At the configured artistic boundary, finish the strongest credible
cut; source, comprehension and technical failures still take the mandatory recovery route.
