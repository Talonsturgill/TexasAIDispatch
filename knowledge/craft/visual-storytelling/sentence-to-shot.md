Use the actual clause, not a vague topic such as research or AI. Name visible objects in everyday terms. Describe the change and its meaning; an object moves is insufficient. If the shot's identity depends on a legend in the director's notes, redesign it.

Support each row with a thumbnail showing start and result. This can be a cheap board sketch or an existing source frame, not a new paid preview. Plan the hardest action and the entire completion path before committing to a treatment.

Do not insist that pictures alone communicate every proper name or date. Require the central action to be visible, and require the combined film to communicate attribution and source limits.

## Existing critic observation sequence

1. View the current rendered treatment at phone size before reading its explanation.
2. Describe the recognized subjects, central change and ending in plain words.
3. Read the narration and identify any clause whose pictured subject, action or relationship is missing, ambiguous or contradictory.
4. Compare the intended meaning with the actual rendered moments. Record exact intervals, not a claim about an unseen component.
5. Return one consolidated correction covering all current defects, preserving accurate narration and alignment where possible.

Keep these findings in the current story_review, phone_observations, blocking_defects or reviewer notes as appropriate. Bind observations to the exact current film through existing review machinery. Do not treat the worksheet's filled cells as proof.

## Rejection examples

- Narration says photo plus depth map; picture shows two unlabeled blocks.
- Narration says a worker chooses a repair; picture shows a floating priority marker without a readable decision.
- Narration says planned improvement; picture silently depicts completed accurate detection.
- Narration says an observed event; picture uses an unrelated stock person or generated reconstruction without disclosure.
- Captions correctly repeat a claim while the principal picture contradicts it.

The owning source/comprehension contract determines the failure classification. Do not invent new scoring weights or waive a comprehension failure at the artistic cap.
