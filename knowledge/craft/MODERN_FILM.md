## Two complete treatments, one executable direction

Before voice, design exactly two feasible, complete alternatives. Each needs a first-frame
question, a recognizable throughline subject, an escalation, a fair source-backed turn, a
closing picture that answers the opening, and a sound arc. Different shot ordering, staging
and visual emphasis must give a viewer a different experience. Two camera poses on the same
tableau are one treatment.

Write `film_direction` with the active policy version, registered episode, actual renderer
hashes, angle, opening promise, performed turn, closing answer, shot timeline and rewards.
The shot timeline covers every story frame, conserves subject identity and source-bound scene
timing, and names an implemented view and overlapping physical event. Use at least three useful
framings. A new action, reaction, comparison, consequence or evidence reveal earns each cut.
An empty push, rotating fan or appearing label alone does not count as new understanding.

Before code review, freeze each shot's `scene_fraction_start` and `scene_fraction_end` within its source
scene, and each reward's `event_fraction` within its physical event. These authored anchors
belong to the approved concept. `board_retime.py` derives the measured seconds from them after
voice alignment, without compounding earlier timing. If the measured read breaks the limits
or event overlap, repair the edit; never stretch the narration or silently move a reward.

The current product limits are in `config/modern_film.json`: first visible change within 1.5
seconds, no shot longer than 5 seconds, and no unearned information interval longer than 4
seconds. These are explicit production constraints chosen after the owner rejection, not a
scientific claim about all audiences. Do not bypass them by relabeling a repeated view or
inventing event declarations. Independent review watches the execution at normal speed.

Generate this edition's fresh hero and supporting artwork under STORY_ART.md before animation.
Finish the hero silhouette, contact shadows, layered surfaces, motivated light and focal hierarchy
at native size and 390-pixel phone width before voice. Use authentic licensed footage when it
provides useful action. Otherwise author a source-bound illustration. Do not infer a particular
failure mechanism, measured value, actual operator, hardware design or completed outcome.

## Perform and judge the complete edit

Narration is an energetic, continuous explanation with purposeful contrasts. Begin the action
immediately, keep connective phrases moving, and reserve emphasis and breathing room for the
turn and answer. Do not put a reverent pause after every clause. Shorten slow writing before
synthesis, rather than accelerating the audio. Compare actual take timing with the approved
shot rewards and listen through the existing audiovisual path. Fast unreadable words fail too.

The existing phone critic and three separate final scorers receive this guide's actual text and
hash. At visual/final scope each returns exact-film `modern_observations` for first frame, visual
progression, shot variety, performed turn, pace, closing answer and finished art. Each contains
an observed interval, concrete finding and pass flag. The ordinary rubric and actual failures
remain unchanged. A high average cannot replace a failed engagement or finish observation.

Optional engineering stops at its reserved boundary. If a mandatory current-route observation
fails, preserve that cut and repair through the configured autonomous completion policy, using
the retained independent source-backed pivot when needed. Neither artistic ceilings nor host
capacity authorize a legacy episode, slideshow rescue or unaudited provider substitution.
