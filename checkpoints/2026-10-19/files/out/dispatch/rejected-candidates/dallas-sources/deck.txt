Contract Terms
The contract includes the cost of purchase, installation, maintenance,
warranty, and replacement support.
• This is a three-year contract for an estimated amount of $2,556,000,
  subject to annual City Council appropriations.
• The contract cost is $852,000 per year, which includes 100 cameras and
  all associated technology, installation, data services, maintenance,
  warranty, and replacement support.
• The City may terminate the contract with 10 days’ written notice at any
  time, without penalty.
• The contract is therefore a service-based, fully inclusive technology
  agreement and not a per-camera equipment purchase model.
