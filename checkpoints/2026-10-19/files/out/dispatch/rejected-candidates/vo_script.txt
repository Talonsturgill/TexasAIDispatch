A Dallas brush truck rolls past a curb pile at street speed.
Its side camera takes a photo, and software flags what it finds.
The city's list includes bulk trash, tires and high grass.
Faces and license plates are blurred.
The city says this is not automated ticketing, and a human is in the loop for all actions.
An analyst reviews the flag, then workers choose no action, a courtesy notice or a case.
A code officer inspects in person before any citation.
A city memo, as Fox 4 reports it, counts fifty seven citations at thirty four properties.
The Dallas Express reports thirty nine of them fell in three southern districts.
Dallas has not finished an evaluation of whether the program works.
A council briefing on the cameras is set for October nineteenth.
