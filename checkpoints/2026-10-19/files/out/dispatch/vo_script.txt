Anyone in San Antonio can now hail a Waymo with no driver, the airport included.
The service began in February in a limited area of sixty square miles.
In April, an empty Waymo was swept into Salado Creek during heavy rain.
Earlier that month, another empty car was stranded in floodwater.
Waymo then filed a software recall for nearly thirty eight hundred cars.
The recall says a car may slow, then drive into standing water on faster roads.
The federal record says the remedy is still under development.
By April twentieth, Waymo had tightened its weather limits as an interim step.
So the airport ride is open, and the record still lists the flood remedy as under development.
