# Make the editorial choice before the picture

Apply this method to newly planned editions from October 3rd, 2026. It guides decisions under the current contracts and does not certify quality. It adds no board schema, scorer, research pass or paid attempt.

## Decide and record

Read the selector and chosen approach dossier in full. For a news report also read the reporting dossier. Use the existing creative_direction and story_contract fields; keep the decision rationale compact and inspectable.

1. **Focus.** In viewer_question and visible_answer, state what changes, for whom, through which sourced action. Name the expected viewer takeaway and the strongest honest limit. Distinguish the narrative answer from an unknown eventual outcome.
2. **Choose.** Begin medium_choice with the named format and approach: News report, Educational explainer or Cinematic scene; then Observed documentary, Explanatory animation, Evidence-led imagery or Cinematic scene. Explain why the available assets can carry the explanation and why a plausible alternative is weaker. Naming an approach is a routing aid, not an approval field.
3. **Order.** Put each beat's reporting job in the existing scene advances and edit notes. For a report choose human-led, mechanism-led or evidence-led ordering. Explain why this evidence appears here and what question it creates or answers. Educational explanation builds a recognizable example through successive states. A cinematic scene follows a task, obstacle, consequential turn and completed action. Do not apply one rundown to all three.
4. **Anticipate confusion.** Name the most likely false inference and the picture or immediate source limit that prevents it. In emotional_turn, connect the viewer's changing understanding to an evidenced stake. Do not invent a person's feelings or claim to know the audience's reaction.
5. **Connect.** In enter_on, leave_on, cut_after_event_id and next_connection, name the visible event, new information and carried subject. Complete the event before the cut. A footage-to-diagram handoff keeps the same identified example or discloses a new one. Finish the sentence-to-shot worksheet inside these existing notes.
6. **Direct and test.** Tie line intent, emphasis, motivated sound and captions to the actual reveal. Before spending, ask what remains understandable with narration muted and editorial labels covered, then test the combined account. Essential dates and attribution can use text; the principal action must read in the picture. Budget the entire remaining review/render path, including independent-provider recovery where required.

These are short decision reasons a colleague can inspect, not a request for a private stream of thought. Use concrete evidence and an alternative that could realistically be built. Do not fill every field with the same sentence, duplicate the research or commission a third treatment.

## Each worker has a distinct decision

- **Researcher:** Identify the most informative picture and the strongest limitation. Recommend an angle that the assets can actually show; flag the sentence for which no truthful picture exists.
- **Director:** Choose the story focus, format, medium and order. Resolve the weak handoff before narration, using the same two-treatment batch and consolidated ledger.
- **VO director:** Translate the approved beat jobs into a continuous performance. Explain a lift, drop or pause through the reveal it supports; preserve the words and alignment.
- **Code critic:** Determine whether the proposed implementation can perform the action and carry the explanation. A planned gesture is not a watched result.
- **Phone critic and scorers:** First describe what is recognizable and what changes in the actual film. Then compare with the approved account. Locate the weakest identity or information handoff and classify the observed defect under the existing criteria. Keep their separate assigned lenses and actual verdicts.

For remote independent recovery, the supplied packet contains the actual method, selector, reporting dossier and named approach text. If a director has not named an approach, all four approach guides are supplied rather than pretending an unread local path was available. The new teaching bundle is added only for editions from October 3rd. A text packet never establishes audiovisual access.

## Why the handoff needs thought

An original film-comprehension study associated event boundaries with meaningful changes in characters, objects, place, goals and causes. Our application is to preserve those relationships across a cut and make a change understandable. This is an editorial inference, not a scientifically established short-video pacing interval or retention guarantee. [Zacks and colleagues, The Brain's Cutting-Room Floor](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/human-neuroscience/articles/10.3389/fnhum.2010.00168/full).

The director can consult the [observed news examples](broadcast-cadence-study.md) when a reporting choice needs a model. Workers use the supplied method, relevant guide and current artifacts without repeatedly reloading the research corpus. Retain real rejections; a well-written plan can't clear absent action, false implication or failed comprehension.
