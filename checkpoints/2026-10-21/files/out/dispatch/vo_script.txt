Chevron has piloted autonomous inspection drones in the Permian Basin, from a company located in Austin.
The companies say AI in the software sends alerts when it detects anomalies to normal operations.
On September 30th, 2025, the Environmental Protection Agency approved the company's Air Max system for periodic methane screening.
The drone carries an infrared camera and follows a flight plan to points of interest.
Then a certified inspector reviews the footage to identify any emissions, which triggers a follow-up inspection.
The approval letter mentions AI only in the title of a protocol.
EPA has also delayed a separate program for large leaks reported by certified third parties until January 22nd, 2027.
On October 7th it said it will propose to rescind that program.
Under this approval, a certified inspector confirms the leak. The letter doesn't say what the AI adds.
