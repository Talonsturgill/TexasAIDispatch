Chevron and Percepto describe AI alerts in their autonomous-drone pilot in West Texas. Under EPA’s separate approval for Percepto’s Air Max system, a certified inspector reviews the infrared footage to identify emissions and trigger a follow-up inspection.

The approval letter mentions AI only in a protocol title. It does not explain what AI adds to that approved process. When you see an AI leak-detection claim, check who confirms the emissions—and distinguish the screening method from EPA’s separate leak-reporting program.
