In the Permian Basin, Chevron tested autonomous drones from a company located in Austin.
The companies say AI alerts when it detects anomalies to normal operations.
On September 30th, 2025, the Environmental Protection Agency approved that company's Air Max drone system for periodic methane screening.
The drone carries an infrared camera and follows a flight plan to points of interest.
Then a certified inspector reviews the footage to identify any emissions, which triggers a follow-up inspection.
The approval letter mentions AI only in the title of a protocol.
EPA also delayed a separate leak reporting program to January 22nd, 2027, and on October 7th said it will propose to rescind it.
