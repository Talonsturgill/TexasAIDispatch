A camera on a tall tower in west Texas watches the land all the way around.
AEP Texas says sixteen of these AI cameras are up, with fifty six planned by February.
When one sees heat or smoke, the software sends a text or an email to the AEP Texas team.
AEP estimates that message lands within ten minutes of detection.
The cameras don't stop fires. AEP says they give it awareness it can share with first responders.
The utility's wildfire lead says no major fire has needed an AEP Texas response since launch.
AEP publishes no record of how much sooner anyone arrives.
The utility's wildfire plan still waits on state approval.
