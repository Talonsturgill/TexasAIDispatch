Several dozen elements can be mixed in roughly a trillion ways.
No person can sort through that many.
On October 22nd, Texas A&M announced a two million dollar grant, to build the Aggie AI Foundry.
The plan is that AI proposes alloys, and computer-controlled robotic arms mix, melt and test them.
Raymundo Arróyave says a computer can predict some behavior, but no computer tool anywhere can predict an alloy's behavior.
So the work has to be done on the actual metal.
The goal is developing alloys in a week or less, not months.
That is the university's goal, not a result. The release gives no opening date.
The AI can suggest. Only the melt can answer.
