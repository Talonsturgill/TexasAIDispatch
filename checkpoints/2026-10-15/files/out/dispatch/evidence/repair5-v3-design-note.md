Minimum widths were checked against 0.21 s over 95 percent of the take 5 clause length, every event passes.
Dry run (no charge, copy in evidence/sim-v3): the v3 board retimed against take 5 words and captions passes documentary_check and modern_film (gates.log). This proves the narrowed fractions satisfy the gate on take 5 timing with margin; the longer clauses of a new take are covered by the caps above.
Runtime: soundcheck --cut compares the take duration with the story runtime plus a 4 percent allowance (MAX_OVERAGE), so a runtime of 60.0 s admits takes up to 62.4 s. The documentary max_gap_s 4.0 limits the tail after the last reward to 4.0 s and the authored closing hold is 1.5 s, so the usable end of speech is about 56.3 s to 58.5 s at runtime 60.0 s. Twelve beats of 0.25 to 0.45 s on take 5's end at 54.66 s project to about 56.7 to 58.7 s. 60.0 s is a projected-range choice, not a source value, and is stated so.
