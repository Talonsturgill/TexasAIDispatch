Customer freight moves. The observer stays aboard.
On October fifth, Volvo and Waabi announced customer operations with Warp in Texas.
Their autonomous truck carries customer freight along the Dallas to Houston corridor, directly to third-party facilities.
Warp groups shipments from different customers. It coordinates routes, capacity and timing.
An observer still sits in the driver's seat. This service has someone aboard.
The companies plan to use these trips to prepare additional routes. Customer freight reaches facilities with the observer aboard.
