Denton held its first hearing on a proposed data-center moratorium, but council took no vote. CBS News Texas reported that all twenty speakers supported the proposed pause. The local proposal would halt acceptance, processing and approval of new applications if adopted. Separately, Governor Greg Abbott directed TCEQ to halt data-center permits pending an ERCOT audit. Denton has scheduled a second hearing for October 27th. The film uses illustrative animation, not footage of the hearing.
