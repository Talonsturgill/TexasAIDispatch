Public Hearing: Data Center Moratorium

Development Notices Posted on August 19, 2026

The City of Denton City Council will hold public hearings regarding a proposed data center moratorium in the City of Denton, Denton County, Texas, and, as applicable, the City of Denton’s extraterritorial jurisdiction. In accordance with Texas Local Government Code Chapter 212.134, the City will hold two public hearings on September 22, 2026, and October 27, 2026, and these public hearing dates were approved by Resolution 26-1287 adopted by City Council.


CC Public Hearing: 
Tuesday, September 22, 2026, 6:30 pm and Tuesday, October 27, 2026, 6:30 pm
City Hall, City Council Chambers
215 E McKinney St
Denton, TX 76201

The meeting is being held in person. For information on how to participate, visit www.cityofdenton.com/publicmeetings.
