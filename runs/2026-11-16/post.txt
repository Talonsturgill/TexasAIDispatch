Why does a whole street in Friendswood walk out to look at the creek every day?

People along Clear Creek still remember Harvey. Since April, seven radar flood sensors from the Galveston County Consolidated Drainage District watch the water with them, and AI tools help predict how a flood could develop on a color-coded public map with text alerts. The decision to stay or go still belongs to the people who live there.
