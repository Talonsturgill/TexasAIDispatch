# What the eye misses

Two visually similar powders frame the question of what a robot knows before handling a material. Si-Ware announced a new Austin US headquarters on September17th. Its application lab is planned, with feasibility testing for physical AI and robotics. A conceptual contact sensor and light cutaway explain absorption signatures, followed by the company-described sugar and flour demonstration and teaching an unknown cream sample. The film closes with an unfilled application test record.

Every scene is an original explanatory illustration. There is no source footage, measured spectral dataset, deployed robot or food-safety finding. The artificial handling stop is editorial caution, not a documented safety function. No independent observation of the company demo is claimed.
