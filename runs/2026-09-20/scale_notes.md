# Visual scale

The fly, eggs and ecological landscape are editorial diagrams with deliberate magnification. They are not physical-size comparisons or a measured geographic model. Their stable teal, coral and ochre roles connect the biological cycle to intervention planning. The wildlife and rancher use the shared cast and fauna drawings. Labels distinguish the research location from a real infestation map.

All source text and captions use the shared font and feed-safe subtitle layout. The closing fly and gate were enlarged after the first completed animatic exposed an unreadable final action. Final phone playback and the exact-film panel determine whether that correction succeeds.
