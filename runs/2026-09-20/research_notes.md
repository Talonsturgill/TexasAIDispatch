# Research selection

Three independent researchers examined fresh Texas movement in farm and ranch, road transport, and weather infrastructure. The USDA ARS September 15th screwworm report provided a verified AI mechanism, current operational use and a Kerrville research connection. The road alternative concerned a Roanoke zoning decision, not a citywide flight ban. The weather alternative described municipal pipe inspection, without proven completed repairs.

The selected source names habitat and ecological preferences, weather conditions and past outbreaks as model inputs. It says the models already inform surveillance, traps and sterile insect releases. Animal transport creates situations they do not predict. Texas Animal Health Commission guidance supplies the owner actions and sterile insect mechanism. Twelve claims were independently checked against complete fetched passages. Promotional success, real-time local forecast and current Kerrville outbreak inferences were excluded.

Primary source snapshots are preserved privately in validation-sources. Public provenance appears in claims.json and the delivery caption.
