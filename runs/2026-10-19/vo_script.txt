Anyone in San Antonio can now hail a Waymo with no driver, the airport included.
The service began in February in a limited area of sixty square miles.
In April, an empty Waymo was swept into Salado Creek.
Earlier that month, another empty car was stranded in floodwater.
Waymo filed a software recall that could affect nearly thirty eight hundred cars.
The recall says the software may let a car slow, then drive into standing water on higher speed roads.
The federal record says the remedy is currently under development.
As an interim step, Waymo tightened its weather limits, and every affected car had the update by April twentieth.
So the airport ride is open, and the federal record lists the flood remedy as currently under development.
