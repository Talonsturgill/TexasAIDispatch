-----BEGIN CAPTION-----
Anyone in San Antonio can now hail a driverless Waymo, the airport included. The service began in February in a limited area of 60 square miles. In April, an empty Waymo was swept into Salado Creek, and Waymo filed a software recall covering nearly 3,800 cars.

The recall says the software may let a car slow, then drive into standing water on higher speed roads. The federal record says the remedy is currently under development. As an interim step, Waymo tightened its weather limits and every affected car had the update by April 20th.
