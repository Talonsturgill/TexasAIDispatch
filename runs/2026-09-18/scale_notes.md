# Scale and contact

The shared resident rig is used at scale1. Contacting robot, person and mug share the same depth plane. The cup handle endpoint is derived from the same pointing-arm solver and palm center used by the rendered hand. It follows that endpoint when the arm lowers and lifts. Release follows completed transfer.

The source sheet and participation door are illustrative graphic props. They are not literal photographs, source-page facsimiles or a deployed user interface. Their smaller framing keeps the person's face and both hands visible.
