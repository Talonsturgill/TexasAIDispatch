# Within reach

A robot can offer a cup and still leave the person reaching. This miniature documentary follows one clearly labeled illustrated mug handoff to explain UT Austin's human and robot co-adaptation research goal.

The current movement is the NSF award term beginning in September. The UT announcement predates that start. This is not an announcement of new funding today. The proposed reach adjustment, simulated apartment demonstration, future consent procedures and community role are kept distinct from a proved care benefit.

The constructed actor and actions are explanatory illustrations, not the people or an observed incident in the university photograph. The film says so from its first frame.
