The magnet inside a blower motor starts as a materials problem.

On September 1st, the University of Houston announced federal support for a research plan that pairs AI predictions of new compounds with AI predictions of how to make them.

Houston researchers will synthesize candidates and measure their magnetic properties. More reliable domestic sourcing is the goal. Commercialization remains the ultimate milestone.

A candidate has to leave the screen before it can turn the fan.
