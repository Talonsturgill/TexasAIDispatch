The blower fan in many modern air conditioners uses a permanent-magnet motor.
The University of Houston wants AI to find new magnet materials.
On September 1st, the university announced federal support for that search.
The plan pairs AI predictions of new compounds with AI predictions of how to make them.
Houston researchers will synthesize the candidates and measure their magnetic properties.
The goal is materials that can be sourced and made more reliably at home.
The plan runs from prediction to synthesis to testing. Commercialization remains the ultimate milestone.
A candidate has to leave the screen before it can turn the fan.
