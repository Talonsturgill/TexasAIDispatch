A pivot moves water across a field. Reading the ground is a different job.

At a free field day near Ralls on September 9th, the Texas Alliance for Water Conservation program includes Autonomous Pivot. Its sensing system rides the irrigation machine. Reflected signals help estimate soil moisture, AI reads crop images, and weather helps shape irrigation advice.

Advice is not a measured result. The announcement reports no measured water-saving result and does not establish a new deployment at the host farm.

The useful next step is a field visit. See the equipment, meet producers and researchers, and bring the questions specific to the ground being irrigated.
