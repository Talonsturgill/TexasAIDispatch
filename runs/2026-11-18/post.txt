On Mars, a call home for help can take up to 44 minutes to come back. So who grabs the medical bag?

At NASA's Johnson Space Center in Houston, Google Public Sector and NASA teams built a proof-of-concept robot for that job. In early lab tests it found and moved a medical bag 85 percent of the time. It is one task in one lab, and the crew medical officer still makes every call.
