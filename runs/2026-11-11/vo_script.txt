IKEA ships furniture from a distribution center in Baytown to its store in Frisco. Later this year, Kodiak AI plans to haul that freight with nobody in the cab.
Before the driver comes out, Kodiak has to finish a safety case.
The driverless stretch is 219 miles, primarily along Interstate 45.
Those miles are part of an established 292 mile route.
A safety case is a stack of claims and evidence. At the end of September, readiness reached 96 percent. At the end of August, it was 93 percent.
By October 30th, Kodiak put that readiness measure at 99 percent.
Kodiak says it is ahead of pace to launch by the end of the year. IKEA is its launch partner.
For now, the empty seat is only a plan.
