The truck that hauls IKEA freight through Texas may soon have an empty seat.

Kodiak AI says it is ahead of pace to launch driverless long haul service by the end of the year, with IKEA as its launch partner. The driverless stretch is 219 miles, mostly Interstate 45, and the company's safety case readiness climbed to 99 percent by October 30th. It is still a plan, and a driver still sits in the cab today.
