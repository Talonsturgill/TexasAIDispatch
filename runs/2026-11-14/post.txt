Robots build the solar table. A person still sets it down.

Near Barry in Navarro County, part of the 457 megawatt Tormes Solar project is being assembled on a factory line parked in the field. Robots fasten panels onto steel tubes, AI-assisted tools check every unit and rovers carry each finished table out. Workers still set every table on its supports.
