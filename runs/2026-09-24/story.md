# The empty seat is an invitation

Aurora's September 23 investor filing offers attendees a ride in a driverless truck on a segment of its active Dallas to Houston commercial route. The film opens with an empty wheel and follows the offer through a route, reported mileage, and McLane's view of driver work.

The turn is documentary evidence: an OFFERED tray fills while a COMPLETED tray stays empty. Aurora reports more than 500,000 cumulative driverless miles, but the cited filing does not document a completed attendee ride or provide an independent comparison with human drivers. The ending returns to the seat and answers the opening question: a passenger could observe an empty wheel on a short ride; that experience would leave routewide safety unanswered.

The truck, worker, route and cab are original illustrations. They show the stated mechanism and an attributed customer view; they do not reconstruct a recorded passenger trip or worker transfer.
