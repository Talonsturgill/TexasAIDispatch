# The seat stays empty

The first image is an empty passenger seat under spinning rotor shadows. The aircraft is real,
the flight campaign starts today, and the absence of a rider is the point.

Joby's piloted electric air taxi spends the week tracing Dallas-Fort Worth routes. A separate
J208 aircraft demonstrates autonomous flight. The two machines stay visually distinct because
the evidence does not permit the film to merge them into one driverless air taxi.

The route line is the film's physical throughline. It leaves an airport flight strip, climbs into
the sky, meets an evidence gate, and then spreads across a Texas map only where the sources permit.
Each later passenger stage sits behind another gate. None opens early.

The earned take is that the empty seat is not the missing product. It is the evidence gate.
Texas has to clear the route before a rider can.

The closing image returns to the empty seat. The route line reaches it but stops at the buckle.
The button asks the viewer to watch the flight log rather than the promise.
