In Fort Worth, the most important passenger is nobody.
Joby's electric air taxi begins a week of Dallas-Fort Worth test flights.
It lifts with a pilot. The autonomous demonstration uses a different aircraft.
That separation matters. The first phase carries no passengers.
The work is not selling a ride. It is tracing and validating a route.
Those flights give the FAA operational evidence for the rules that come next.
The proposed network runs from Dallas toward Austin, San Antonio, and eventually Houston.
Only later, under FAA oversight, could the ladder move from pilot only to non-paying riders, then paying ones.
So the empty seat is not a missing product. It is the evidence gate.
Before a rider can enter, the route must clear. The flight log matters more than the promise.
