# The rock goes in

SMU plans a mineral-testing facility at NOV's existing drilling test center in Navasota. The proposal would place engineered mineral-bearing rock inside existing wells for repeated drilling, sensing and automation tests. Students and workers would train with AI, digital twins and hands-on practice.

The Energy Department selected the project for award negotiations. The announcements report no completed testbed or test results.

All machinery and underground views are original proposal illustrations. The specimen returns visibly drilled while a separate specimen enters for the next test. No successful extraction, economic ore value or measured sensor result is asserted.
