# The clock that has not started

Brownsville commissioners approved a resolution directing the city manager to begin the process for a temporary data-center moratorium. That is a real movement in the public record. It is not the same thing as an enacted moratorium, and the film makes the gap between those states its physical subject.

The city says its Unified Development Code carries no specific data-center regulations. The proposed process buys time to study electricity and water, noise, land-use compatibility, infrastructure, and possible economic and fiscal effects. The proposal also calls for reactivating the Environmental and Conservation Task Force to study the facilities and recommend rules to the commission.

The reported administrator proposal names select October and November dates: another hearing on October 6, a first ordinance reading on October 13, and a second reading and final vote on November 17. Those dates are proposed, not guaranteed, and they are not a claim that the report names the complete statutory sequence. If the ordinance passes on that proposed schedule, the initial moratorium would begin November 18.

The city says an initial pause can last up to ninety days and may be extended once for another ninety days after additional required proceedings, for a maximum of one hundred eighty days. The turn is the clock face: the hearing hand is moving through public steps while the moratorium hand remains marked NOT IN EFFECT. The city posts commission videos and offers online registration for public comments.
