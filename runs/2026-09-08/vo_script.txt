Brownsville voted to start a data-center moratorium process. The moratorium itself did not start.
Brownsville's code has no specific data-center rules, city staff said.
The city wants to study electricity, water, noise, land use, infrastructure, and fiscal effects.
The proposal calls for reactivating an environmental task force to study data centers and recommend rules.
Administrators proposed another hearing, two readings, and a November seventeenth final vote.
If the ordinance passes on the proposed schedule, the ninety-day clock would begin November eighteenth.
The city says an additional ninety-day extension is possible after further proceedings.
So the hearing process is moving. The moratorium is still not in effect.
