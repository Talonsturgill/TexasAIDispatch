A leak under a street can hide for months. Arlington went looking for it from orbit.

In a pilot, satellites scanned the whole city and an algorithm turned the scan into about 340 possible leak spots. A city crew still had to go out, confirm each one and dig. The city counts 34 leaks found and repaired, and an estimated 1,130,567 gallons of water saved.
