-----BEGIN CAPTION-----
The nearest ER often has no trauma surgeon on call.

In a small Texas emergency room, deciding to transfer a badly hurt patient takes 15 to 30 minutes, and Texas registry data show up to 80 percent of the most severely injured wait more than two hours to be transferred. A UT Health San Antonio trauma surgeon co-leads a statewide team building an AI tool that flags severe injury and prompts that call sooner. It is still headed for a pilot, and it is meant to sharpen the doctor's judgment, not replace it.
