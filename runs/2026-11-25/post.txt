Two cameras on a Panhandle tower spot the smoke before anyone else does.

After the Smokehouse Creek fire, the largest wildfire in Texas history, Xcel Energy and Pano AI put AI camera stations on towers across the Panhandle. Each one sweeps a full circle every minute. A person checks every flag, two towers cross their sightlines to pin the spot, and the call goes to 911. Plenty of alerts still turn out to be nothing.
