Who tightens the drill pipe now?

On ExxonMobil's automated rigs in Midland, three robot arms supplied by Helmerich and Payne move and connect pipe weighing roughly 2,000 pounds, the work floor crews used to do by hand in the red zone. The crew moves up to a small office on the rig and runs the robots from a screen. Exxon expects similar staffing and believes the robots could prevent about a third of high-consequence incidents, and both are company expectations, not counts.
