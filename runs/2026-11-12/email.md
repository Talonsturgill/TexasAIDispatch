The drone can point. Somebody still has to walk out and look.

In June, federal agencies flew drones over Jim Wells County to show how cameras and AI could find livestock hit by the screwworm. Officials call technology one part of the plan and the sterile fly program the most effective weapon. This illustrated film follows one ranch hand and one detection box through brush that hides most of what it needs to find.
