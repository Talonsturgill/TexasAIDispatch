Up to three hours of paperwork for one student's plan.

At Mesquite ISD, east of Dallas, special education teachers use an AI tool built with Panorama Education that pulls a student's data into a draft IEP and lights up the boxes a teacher may have forgotten. Teachers reported drafting fell to about 45 minutes per student. It won't decide what help a student needs, and a privacy advocate says families should be told up front.
