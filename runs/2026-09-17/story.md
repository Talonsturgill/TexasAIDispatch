# After the fax arrives

A medical fax reaches a clinic before it reaches the right chart. On September 16th, UTHealth Houston reported on iDFAX, an AI system used in its clinical operations to extract document details and connect incoming faxes to patient records. A technical account describes classification and document splitting as well.

The film follows an anonymous illustrated document from manual filing through identity extraction and record association. It closes with the filed chart and a patient still separated by an unanswered question. The university reports saved staff time. Its new public account gives no appointment waiting-time measurements, so the film does not claim earlier care.
