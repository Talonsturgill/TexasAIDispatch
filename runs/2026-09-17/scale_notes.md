# Illustrative scale

The fax, chart, desk and report are diagram units drawn at deliberately varied scale for phone comprehension. They are not a measured reconstruction of UTHealth space, paper dimensions, software screens or clinical machinery. Harris County maps to the gulf region. No animal or habitat claim is present. The clinic light and abstract skyline support place without inventing a named building. The human/desk relationship is visually inspected in the actual frames; board_scale_check records the remaining unmeasured kind debt honestly.
