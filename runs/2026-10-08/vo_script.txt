An AI clue still needed a living test.
In Houston, Texas Children's and Baylor researchers found a promising gene change after standard analysis of a parent and child came up empty.
AI-MARRVEL pointed to BRSK1. Flies missing its equivalent gene had trouble moving.
A normal human gene largely restored movement. Patient variants restored only part of it.
That comparison supports a likely diagnosis for these families.
The AI picked a candidate. The flies helped test it. This is evidence about a disorder, not a treatment.
