## The earned take

The important line in this self-driving laboratory is not between people and machines. It is
between routine work the loop can repeat and judgment the researchers still have to supply.

That sentence comes directly from the award abstract's division of labor. Automation handles
fabrication, characterization and routine computation. Researchers focus on hypothesis generation,
interpretation and metallurgical decisions. The film performs that division instead of explaining
it over unrelated laboratory scenery.
