One scan in a long line can't wait. Which one?

At Houston Methodist in the Texas Medical Center, an AI tool called Aidoc reads incoming scans, paints a heat map on a possible problem and moves that exam to the top of the radiologist's worklist. The radiologist still reads it and makes the call, and the hospital says plainly that the tool isn't perfect.
