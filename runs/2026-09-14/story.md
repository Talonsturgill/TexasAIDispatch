# The case still has work

Kodiak reported progress in its highway safety case on September 8th. The percentage describes company evidence completion, not crash probability, independent certification or a driverless launch. The June filing names Texas as the initial planned market. Existing Dallas to Houston freight is context only. The company still targets a year-end driverless long-haul launch and says engineering verification and validation work remains.

The editorial picture is a paper road whose unfinished gap folds back into a safety case. It is a schematic, not a reconstruction of a company office, a real damaged road, a known system failure, or a launch route. The existing-service truck is distinct from the planned driverless case.
