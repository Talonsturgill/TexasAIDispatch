# Captions and the Docket sign-off

The shared subtitle track now sets complete measured cues in larger, bold Manrope, balances
the line lengths and fits each dark backplate to the actual text advance. A warm vertical rule
ties the captions to the publication. The whole cue appears at its measured start, with a small
settling motion and no artificial word clock, typewriter reveal or lost reading time. Dense cues
fail with an instruction to use measured phrase boundaries instead of turning into fine print.

The shared closing card uses the existing Texas AI Docket Lone Star flag and Fraunces masthead,
a prominent invitation to visit texasaidocket.com, and a separate source/music colophon. The
flag geometry and colours follow the sibling Docket repository's scripts/site/mark.py and
config/brand.yaml. This adapts the existing identity into motion; it does not create a new logo.
Only the masthead settles into place. All source and music attribution remains visible for the
entire credit tail, with URLs preserved and text constrained above the feed overlay.

The daily authoring routine now requires these shared components in new episodes and asks
reviewers to inspect a long cue and the end card in the phone feed.

## Reference

This is a design replay of the September 16th film, The rock goes in. Its narration, measured
caption text and times, story animation and complete source/music credit text are unchanged.
The published episode and its existing draft are not replaced. The new design applies to
future renders through the shared production engine.

The preview and stills in this directory demonstrate the final rendering. Validation and exact
media/source hashes are recorded in verification.json.
